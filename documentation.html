Multi-Deck Signal Designer: Documentation

Global Controls | 4-Phase Deck | 3-Phase Deck

This document explains how each control manipulates the math and motion of the signal outputs in simple, everyday terms.

Global Controls

▶ Start Master Recording

What it does: Begins listening to all active decks and logs their movements into a master file.

Output Effect: Doesn't change the physical feeling. When you click stop, it packages the timeline of everything you just did into a `.json` file that you can load into the Playback Engine later to perfectly recreate the session.

Advanced Video Beat Sync

What it does: Analyzes a loaded video's audio track (or visual flashes) to automatically detect the "BPM" or tempo.

Output Effect: Allows you to map the speed of your decks directly to the video. Energy mode listens for loud kicks/bass. Transient mode listens for sharp clicks/claps. Visual mode tracks brightness changes inside a little draggable target box.

4-Phase Deck Controls

The 4-Phase deck calculates independent intensities for 4 separate outputs (E1, E2, E3, E4) based on geometric coordinate mapping.

Pattern / Macro Selector

What it does: Chooses the "path" the power dot travels across the grid.

Output Effect: Sequences (like Round Robin or Pendulum) are moving paths that create a continuous swirling or sweeping sensation. Macros lock the signal into static, hard-coded combinations (like toggling just the left and right sides) without needing to move the dot.

Speed (Hz) Syncable

What it does: Controls how fast the chosen Pattern completes one full cycle.

Output Effect: 1.00 Hz means the pattern loops exactly once per second. Check the "Sync" box to let the Video Beat Sync panel automatically push detected tempo changes directly to this slider.

Orbit Radius (%) X/Y Split Available

What it does: Controls how far from the center the power dot reaches during its motion.

Output Effect: At 100%, the dot reaches the very edges of the grid (maximum intensity swapping). At 20%, it stays tightly bundled near the center (a much softer, more diffuse sensation). Unchecking "Sync Axes" lets you make the circle into a squished oval (e.g., wide horizontal reach, shallow vertical reach).

Sweep Arc & End Pause

What it does: Specifically modifies the "Pendulum" patterns.

Output Effect: Sweep Arc limits how far the pendulum swings (e.g., 180° is a half-circle windshield wiper). End Pause tells the dot to briefly stop and hold its position at the edge before swinging back (measured in milliseconds).

Rotation Angle (Deg)

What it does: Tilts the entire mathematical grid under the power dot.

Output Effect: If your hardware outputs feel physically "crooked" or misaligned, turning this slider will rotate the feeling (up to 180 degrees left or right) until it aligns properly with reality.

Engine Mode (Geometric vs Intensity)

What it does: Changes how the math translates the dot's X/Y position into output power.

Output Effect: Geometric is raw and accurate; if the dot is exactly between A and B, A and B are both getting 50% power. Intensity Modulated applies a smoothing curve so the feeling fades more softly as it moves between points.

LFO (Advanced Auto-Motion)

What it does: An invisible "hand" that slowly pushes the center anchor point of your pattern around the grid.

Output Effect: If you are running a tight, fast circle in the middle of the grid, applying a slow Horizontal LFO will cause that entire spinning circle to slowly drift from the left side of the grid to the right side, and back again.

ReStim Modifiers (C0, P0, etc.)

What it does: Advanced variables that modify the internal electrical shape of the signal.

Output Effect:
• C0 (Carrier Freq): The fundamental "pitch" of the buzz (higher is sharper, lower is a deep hum).
• P0/P1 (Pulse Freq/Width): Changes the signal from a continuous hum into a "tapping" or "thumping" feeling.

3-Phase Deck Controls

The 3-Phase deck uses an entirely different architecture, outputting Cartesian coordinates (L0 for X-axis, L1 for Y-axis) and a Carrier Frequency modifier (C0) to drive a three-point mixing engine.

▶ Start Deck

What it does: Activates the math engines for both the Left and Right channels simultaneously.

Output Effect: You will immediately see the power dot start generating a trace path on the canvas, and TCode will start streaming to your hardware.

Orbit Radius (%)

What it does: Adjusts the global size of the movement.

Output Effect: Scales both the Left and Right channel outputs down. Lower percentages keep the motion closer to the center (neutral) point of the grid.

Orbit Speed / AM Freq (Hz) Linked Channels

What it does: AM (Amplitude Modulation) is what actually moves the dot. The Left Channel controls Up/Down motion, and the Right Channel controls Left/Right motion.

Output Effect:
• Linked: By designating a "Base" channel and setting a "Multiplier", the math forces the dot to draw perfect geometric shapes (like circles, infinity signs, or figure-eights).
• Unlinked: You can set the speeds entirely independently, resulting in chaotic, drifting, bouncing-DVD-logo style movement.

Channel Frequencies & FM Depth

What it does: Controls the "pitch" (Carrier Frequency, C0) of the signal when the dot is in different areas.

Output Effect: You can make it so the feeling is a deep hum when the dot is on the left side of the screen, and a sharp buzz when the dot is on the right side. FM Depth and FM Freq allow you to make the pitch warble up and down like a siren while it moves.

Global Pulse (P0/P1)

What it does: Overrides the continuous feeling with a rhythmic tapping.

Output Effect: Pulse Freq determines how many taps happen per second. Pulse Width determines how long each individual tap lasts (a very low width feels like a sharp needle prick, a higher width feels like a solid push).

V0 Dual Tremolo

What it does: Modulates the Master Volume (V0) in rhythmic waves.

Output Effect: Creates a "swelling" and "fading" feeling on top of whatever the dot is currently doing. You can run two separate tremolos (Alpha and Beta) at different speeds at the same time to create complex, unpredictable volume swells.

Spatial Auto-Motion

What it does: An advanced feature that forces the center point of the grid to drift around automatically.

Output Effect: Similar to the 4-Phase LFO. If you change "Power Dot Motion" to Pendulum, you can combine a swinging pendulum motion that is also slowly orbiting around the outside of the grid.
